About the lab.

Elemental Research works at the boundary of world-model research and interpretability. We develop tools for inspecting model behavior and pursue questions that can be tested through experiments.

APPROACH

Make claims inspectable

A useful explanation should survive more than a visual inspection. We look for evidence that an internal signal changes a model’s behavior, then make the experiment possible to examine again.

Observe

Record representations, predictions, and uncertainty as a model processes an environment or sequence.

Intervene

Replay trajectories and alter a specific state or input to test whether a proposed mechanism matters.

Evaluate

Compare the resulting behavior across examples, including cases where a model’s prediction fails.
