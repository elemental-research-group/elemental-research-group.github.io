Making AI systems understandable.

Elemental Research Lab studies how AI systems form representations, make predictions, and use information.

We turn research questions into tools for inspecting model behavior, and share our work so others can examine and build on it.

World models

How do predictive systems represent and reason about their environment?

Interpretability

What computations and representations sit behind a model's behavior?

Model diagnostics

Do observed signals reflect what actually drives a prediction?

Approach

A question first. A trace second. A claim only after.

We build the means to inspect a system while we study it. This keeps research questions connected to observable behavior.

QUESTION

Form the question

Start with a precise uncertainty about representation, prediction, or behavior.

INSTRUMENT

Make it inspectable

Capture internal states, replay trajectories, and compare what changes under intervention.

TEST

Test the explanation

Look for evidence that survives alternate examples and measurements.

SHARE

Share the artifact

Release tools, drafts, and methods that others can examine and extend.
