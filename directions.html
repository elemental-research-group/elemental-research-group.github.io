Four connected directions.

How systems build, use, and revise a model of their environment and how we inspect the result.

WORLD MODELS

Learning how the world works.

How can a model learn an environment's changing state, predict what happens next, and account for the effect of an action?

Represent · Predict · Simulate
INTERPRETABILITY

Making the model legible.

What has a model actually learned inside, and which internal signals drive a prediction? We build tools to make those questions testable.

Explore WorldModelLens
EMBEDDED WORLD MODELS

Testing a plan before acting.

What changes when a world model sits inside an AI agent, letting the agent simulate possible outcomes before choosing a step?

Model · Compare · Decide
CONTINUAL LEARNING

Keeping the picture current.

How can a model keep learning from new data after training, especially when the environment itself changes?

Update · Adapt · Recheck
Research questions

What would change our minds?

Each direction needs a testable account of what a model knows and how that knowledge affects behavior.

REPRESENTATION

Does the internal state carry the relevant information?

Compare what a model encodes with what it predicts across changing environments.

CAUSALITY

Does a proposed explanation change the result?

Use interventions and alternative trajectories to separate correlation from influence.

GENERALIZATION

Does the account survive a new setting?

Look for explanations that hold beyond the examples used to find them.

Research approach

From observations to understanding.

We study how models turn observations into internal representations and predictions. By tracing and comparing internal states, we can test which signals actually shape a model's behavior.
