Research directions.

We study how AI systems build a picture of the world, use it to make decisions, and update it when the world changes.

RESEARCH

Four connected areas

World models

How do models learn changing environments and predict what happens next?

Interpretability

What do internal representations encode, and which signals drive a prediction?

Embedded world models

How can an agent use a model to compare possible actions before acting?

Continual learning

How can a model adapt as new evidence and changing environments arrive?

APPROACH

Observe, test, compare

We inspect model states, replay behavior, and intervene on internal signals to test explanations against evidence.
